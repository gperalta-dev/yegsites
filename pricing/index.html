    Questions

    Do I pay anything to see my preview?

    No. I build it from your Google listing and send you the link. You look at it on your phone, ask for changes, or say no thanks. You pay only if you keep it.

    Is the domain included?

    Yes. The first year of your own domain is included in the one-time price: I suggest a few names, you pick. After that, the renewal is covered by your monthly plan, and the domain is yours. If you ever leave, I transfer it to you.

    What if I don't like it?

    Then it doesn't go live and you don't pay. I change it until you say it's right, and only then does it get published.

    What does the monthly price cover?

    Hosting, and small edits when you need them: new hours, photos, prices, a holiday notice. Add-ons with a monthly price (review texts, missed-call text back, the chat assistant) are on top. You can cancel any month.

    I already use Square, Fresha or Booksy. Does that work?

    Yes. Online booking hooks up to the app you already use, so your calendar stays where it is.

    Who owns the website?

    You do. If you ever leave, I hand over the files and transfer the domain to you.

    Are taxes included?

    No. Prices are in Canadian dollars, before tax.
