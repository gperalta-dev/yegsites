Questions.

Do I really own it?

Yes. The site is yours. If you ever leave, you get the files and I help you move it.

What if I already have Instagram?

Keep it. People who search Google want hours, a map and a call button, and Google shows websites for that. Your site links to your Instagram.

Do I need a domain?

Not to start. If you want one like yourshop.ca, I'll set it up in your name and you pay the yearly fee directly.

What do you need from me?

Nothing to see the preview. To go live: a few photos and anything I got wrong about your hours or services.

Do I have to pay every month?

The $20 a month keeps your site online and covers small edits. Cancel any month with no fee. You keep the site files and can host them anywhere.

Where are you?

Edmonton. I'm happy to meet at your shop to go over the preview.
