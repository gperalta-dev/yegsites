Two things, done properly.

Pizza from the wood oven and pasta made in the same kitchen. A short menu means everything on it is cooked often and cooked well.

  • Oak and maple in the oven, nothing else
  • Pasta cut fresh before every service
  • Gluten-free crust on request, made separately

The room

A long room, a short bar and candles on every table. It gets loud in the best way after seven.

Private dining

The back room holds one long table for birthdays, rehearsal dinners and team nights. Family-style menus, set together with you ahead of time.
