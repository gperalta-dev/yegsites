Water coming through the ceiling?

Do these three things first. They take two minutes and can save the floor.

Call the emergency line
  1. Shut off the main valveUsually where the water line comes through the basement wall, near the meter. Turn it clockwise until it stops.
  2. Turn off the water heaterGas: set the dial to pilot. Electric: switch it off at the panel.
  3. Call us, day or nightTell us what you see. We'll tell you what to do while we're on the way.

What we fix and install

Leaks and burst pipes

Kitchen and bathroom plumbing
