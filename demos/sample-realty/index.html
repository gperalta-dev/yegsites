Questions buyers ask.

Yours isn't here? Ask the chat, bottom right.

How big is the house?

2,250 sq ft over two storeys, with 4 bedrooms, 3.5 bathrooms and a double attached garage.

Is the backyard fenced?

Yes, all the way around, with a deck off the back door. The last part of the tour walks out to it.

What's the bonus room?

A room over the garage with space for a sectional, a TV and a desk by the window. It works as a home office or a playroom.

Can I see it in person?

On a real listing, yes: book a showing and the agent confirms a time. This one is a demo, so there's nothing to visit.

How much do I need for a down payment?

At least 5% of the first $500,000 and 10% of the rest: $39,990 at this sample price. With less than 20% down the mortgage needs loan insurance, 2.80% to 4.00% of the loan with CMHC, added to the mortgage.

Is there a land transfer tax in Alberta?

No. Alberta charges Land Titles fees instead: $50 plus $5 for every $5,000 of the price, and the same again on the mortgage. On this home that's about $1,300 to $1,400 in all.

Can I pay it off over 30 years?

With 20% or more down, usually, if your lender offers it. With less than 20% down, 30 years is only for first-time buyers and new builds, and the insurance costs 0.20% more. Otherwise it's 25 years.

What else should I budget for?

A lawyer for the purchase, a home inspection, moving, and setting up utilities. Prices vary, so ask for quotes before you make an offer.

Is this a real home?

No. Sample Realty and this listing are made up. Downtown, the river valley and both bridges follow real Edmonton data. The suburb and the house are 3D renders, and the rooms inside were made with AI.
